# A-Boundary-Point-Estimate-to-Approach-Large-Language-Models-Internal-Representation
This repo contains the python implementation of the boundary point detection method we developed in the corresponding paper. 
In short, the BoS(x) is computed based on the nearest neighbors (nn) of x. For k=12-nn, the algorithm will select two nn among the k ones such that the angle formed between the vector k_1-x and k_2-x is the highest among all possible pairwise angles, and such that k_1-x and k_2-x are not collinear to be able to form a basis for the Gram-Schmidt projection applied on the 10 nn (12-2 = 10). The BoS is then the count of projected nn that fall inside the angle formed by k_1-x and k_2-x. A BoS near one means that x sees most of its neighbors in front of him and is therefore positioned on the border of its neighborhood. A BoS close to 0 means that most neighbors of x are behind him (outside of its angle) and hence x is also placed at the border of its neighborhood. A BoS near 0.5 refers to interior points.
The initial variant is named _pipeline_initial_ and requires data points and the specification of the number of k-nn.
The second variant defined as _pipeline_adjusted_ requires the data points, k-nn and the arbitrary threshold _prop_thresh_ deciding when to recompute the BoS to account for concavity situations. 
The third variant defined as _pipeline_oriented_ requires the data points, k-nn and the arbitrary tolerance of the orientation of the maximum angle toward the barycenter of the point cloud.
